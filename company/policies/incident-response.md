# Incident Response Plan

Version 1.6 · Owner: Head of Platform · Approved 2025-09-15

- Severity levels SEV1–SEV3 with an on-call incident commander.
- Customers affected by a confirmed security incident involving their data
  are notified **within 72 hours** of confirmation.
- The plan is exercised with a tabletop at least once a year (see
  `evidence/ir-tabletop-2026-02.md`).
