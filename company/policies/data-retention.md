# Data Retention and Deletion

Version 1.3 · Owner: Legal & Privacy (Rosa Lindqvist) · Approved 2025-10-01

- Customer statement files and ledger data are kept for the term of the contract.
- When a contract ends, customer data is deleted within **30 days**, and
  backups age out within the backup retention period.
- Reconciliation exports expire automatically after 30 days.
- All customer data is stored in the United States (AWS us-east-1).
