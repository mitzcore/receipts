# Vendor Management and Subprocessors

Version 1.1 · Owner: Legal & Privacy · Approved 2025-10-01

New vendors that will process customer data need a security review and a
signed DPA **before** any customer data is sent to them.

## Current subprocessors (customer data)

| Subprocessor | Purpose | Location |
|---|---|---|
| Amazon Web Services | Hosting, storage | US (us-east-1) |
| Sentry | Error monitoring (scrubbed of PII) | US |
| Postmark | Transactional email | US |
