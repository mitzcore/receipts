# Access Control Policy

Version 2.4 · Owner: Head of Platform · Approved 2025-09-15

1. Every workforce account (employees **and contractors**) signs in through
   Okta SSO.
2. **Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for every workforce account.**
   No exceptions are granted for accounts that can reach production or
   customer data.
3. Passwords are at least 14 characters and are checked against breached-password lists.
4. Production access is role-based and least-privilege. Admin access is
   limited to the Engineering group.
5. Access is reviewed every quarter. Leavers are deprovisioned within 24 hours.
